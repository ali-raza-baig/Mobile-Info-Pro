What is a reconditioned engine?

A reconditioned engine is a used engine that has been completely disassembled, cleaned, inspected, and rebuilt using new parts where needed. Every reconditioned engine in our network undergoes a 7-step process:

  1. Full Diagnostic & Strip Down: Scanned for faults, compression tested, and stripped for full inspection
  2. Ultrasonic Cleaning & Crack Testing: Deep-cleaned using ultrasonic tanks; blocks and heads tested for cracks
  3. Precision Machining: Heads resurfaced, crankshafts polished, cylinders bored to exact tolerances
  4. OEM Part Replacement: New pistons, rings, valve seals, timing components, and bearings
  5. Reassembly & Bench Testing: Tested for oil pressure, temperature stability, and emissions compliance
  6. Final Calibration & Delivery: Calibrated, labeled, and delivered with a report and warranty
